Basira — Terms of Service

Last updated: 2026-09-14

What Basira is

Basira is a private Windows desktop application operated by an independent social media manager (contact: saadhassun37@gmail.com). It reads the performance statistics of accounts the manager handles for his clients and shows them to the manager and that client. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

Who may connect an account

An account is connected only by its owner, or by a person the platform itself authorizes to grant access, through the platform's official authorization screen. By connecting an account you confirm that you have that right.

What Basira does and does not do

  • Basira requests read-only permissions and uses the data only to produce reports and comparisons about the connected account.
  • Basira never publishes, edits or deletes content, never sends messages, and never creates or changes advertising campaigns or budgets.
  • Data is stored only on the manager's computer and is handled as described in the Privacy Policy.

Platform terms

Use of each platform through Basira is also subject to that platform's own terms, including the YouTube Terms of Service, the Meta Platform Terms and the TikTok Developer Terms.

Ending access

You can revoke Basira's access at any time from the platform's own settings, or ask by email for your data to be deleted. Authorized data is deleted within 7 days.

No warranty

Basira shows statistics as returned by the platforms, with their source and time of reading. It does not guarantee account growth or results, and figures may differ briefly from platform dashboards while the platforms process data.

Changes

Changes to these terms are published on this page with a new date.
